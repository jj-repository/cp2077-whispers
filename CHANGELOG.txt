0.1.3
- New phone contact WHISPERS: at character level 5 it texts you once about the website NETdir://nc.whispers (checked
  when you enter a district or step out of a vehicle). The contact stays in your phone for 3 game days, read or not,
  then disappears for good. Not sent if you already opened the site.
0.1.2
- Website readable: everything bigger, the place line light on dark instead of dark grey. Two tabs: TODAY (the day's
  whispers + ask around) and STILL OUT THERE (every open whisper, newest first, 4 per page with NEWER / OLDER), so
  nothing runs off the bottom of the page. Smaller header picture.
- Prices: every daily whisper is now paid, each its own tip, rolled once between 50 and 300 eddies and saved with the
  game. Ask around starts at 100 and doubles with every ask (100, 200, 400 ...); 24 game hours after the first ask it
  is back to 100. The button shows the current price. Whispers already posted free in 0.1.1 stay free.
- Settings: whisper price lowest / highest, ask around start price, ask around reset (game hours). The old tip-lock
  chance / fixed prices are gone.
- Map pins are the game's own "?" (undiscovered) icon instead of the quest-giver icon, on the world map, the minimap
  and in the world.
0.1.1
- City gems open right after the first mission ("The Rescue", vanilla fact q001_mission0_complete) instead of
  later in Act 1. Saves already past that point see them at once.

0.1.0
- First release. The game's hidden gems (194 in the city, 46 in Dogtown with Phantom Liberty) as street rumours on
  the website NETdir://nc.whispers: a few whispers per game day (some tip-locked), "ask around" for one more, a map
  pin for every revealed whisper. The street may whisper on its own when you enter a district or step out of a
  vehicle near a hidden gem (20 %, 250 m, once per game hour). Cases close when you read the shard, open the mail,
  pick up the weapon or reach the spot. Rewards every 10 cases and for all of them.
- Settings menu (Mod Settings): whispers per day, tip-lock chance, prices, street chance / radius / cooldown,
  Dogtown on/off, rewards and payouts.
- Progress from Missing Persons - Fixer's Hidden Gems / Pacifica Typhoon - Dogtown's Hidden Gems carries over on the
  first load (finished gems count as closed, they don't pay again). Those two mods are incompatible: remove them.
