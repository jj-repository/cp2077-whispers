0.1.1
- City gems open right after the first mission ("The Rescue", vanilla fact q001_mission0_complete) instead of
  later in Act 1. Saves already past that point see them at once.

0.1.0
- First release. The game's hidden gems (194 in the city, 46 in Dogtown with Phantom Liberty) as street rumours on
  the website NETdir://nc.whispers: a few whispers per game day (some tip-locked), "ask around" for one more, a map
  pin for every revealed whisper. The street may whisper on its own when you enter a district or step out of a
  vehicle near a hidden gem (20 %, 250 m, once per game hour). Cases close when you read the shard, open the mail,
  pick up the weapon or reach the spot. Rewards every 10 cases and for all of them.
- Settings menu (Mod Settings): whispers per day, tip-lock chance, prices, street chance / radius / cooldown,
  Dogtown on/off, rewards and payouts.
- Progress from Missing Persons - Fixer's Hidden Gems / Pacifica Typhoon - Dogtown's Hidden Gems carries over on the
  first load (finished gems count as closed, they don't pay again). Those two mods are incompatible: remove them.
