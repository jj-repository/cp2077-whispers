WHISPERS OF NIGHT CITY
Street talk, overheard and resold. Every whisper marks a spot on your map.

Night City is full of hidden gems: a body nobody reported, a stash nobody came back for, a drone that fell out of the sky. This mod turns all of them into street rumours. A website posts a few whispers every day, each one a pin on your map with a vague line and a rough location. Go there, find out what happened, close the case.

194 hidden gems in the city, plus 46 in Dogtown with Phantom Liberty.


FEATURES

A text from WHISPERS
- At level 5 a contact called WHISPERS texts you about the website, so you find it in-game.
- The contact stays in your phone for 3 game days, read or not, then disappears.
- Not sent if you already opened the site.

The website
- Open NETdir://nc.whispers on any computer.
- Three new whispers every game day, each behind its own tip (50-300 eddies).
- "Ask around" for an extra whisper any time: 100 eddies, doubling with every ask, back to 100 one game day after your first ask.
- A second tab lists every whisper still out there.

The map
- Only gems you've heard about get a pin, shown as a "?" icon.
- Hover the pin to read the rumour and the place.

The street talks
- Entering a district or stepping out of a vehicle has a 20% chance to reveal the nearest hidden gem within 250 m. At most once per game hour.

Closing a case
- Shard gems: pick up and read the shard.
- Dogtown mail gems: open the mail on the computer.
- Dogtown iconic weapons: pick up the weapon.
- Spot gems (no shard): at the revealed spot, pick something up, step out of a vehicle or enter the district there. Just walking past doesn't count.

Rewards
- Every 10 closed cases: crafting components + 150 eddies.
- Close them all: 3000 eddies, legendary components and Comrade's Hammer.

Fair gates
- City gems unlock after "The Rescue" (Watson first, the rest after The Heist).
- Dogtown gems unlock once Dogtown opens.
- Gems made impossible by your choices or lifepath are dropped, never counted against you.

Performance
- No timers, no polling, nothing per frame. Everything runs on game events.


SETTINGS (Mod Settings, optional)
Esc > Mods > Whispers of Night City
- Whispers per day, lowest/highest whisper price
- Ask-around start price and reset hours
- Street reveal chance, radius and cooldown
- Dogtown gems on/off
- Rewards on/off and payout amounts
Without Mod Settings everything runs on the defaults above.


REQUIREMENTS
- RED4ext
- redscript
- Codeware
- Browser Extension
- Optional: Mod Settings
- Optional: Phantom Liberty (Dogtown gems, detected automatically)


INCOMPATIBLE
- Missing Persons - Fixer's Hidden Gems
- Pacifica Typhoon - Dogtown's Hidden Gems
They cover the same gems and would pin and pay them twice. Remove them before installing.

Your progress carries over: on first load, gems you finished in those mods count as closed (no double payout), and open gigs show up as whispers on your map.


INSTALL
Extract the zip into your game folder (the one with bin, archive and r6).

UNINSTALL
Delete r6\scripts\WhispersNC\ and archive\pc\mod\WhispersNC.archive.
The mod only stores a few quest facts in your save; they are harmless without it. Map pins are not saved, so nothing is left behind.


NOTES
- All rumour lines and location texts are written for this mod.
- One gem (a shard removed from the game in 2.0) is never offered.
