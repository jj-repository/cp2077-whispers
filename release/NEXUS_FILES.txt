MOD NAME
Whispers of Night City

SUMMARY (max 350 characters)
Night City's hidden gems as street rumours. A website posts a few whispers every day, each one a pin on your map: a body, a stash, a story nobody told you about. Read the shard, close the case. 194 gems in the city, 46 in Dogtown with Phantom Liberty. The street may whisper on its own when you walk by.
