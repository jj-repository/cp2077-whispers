# Whispers of Night City (Cyberpunk 2077)

The game's hidden gems - the bodies, stashes, lost drones and little stories CDPR tucked into the world (194 in the
city, 46 in Dogtown with Phantom Liberty) - turned into street rumours. A website, `NETdir://nc.whispers`, posts a few
whispers every game day; each one puts a pin on the map with a vague line and a rough location. Find the spot, read
the shard, close the case. Walk the city and the street may whisper on its own.

Needs RED4ext, redscript, Codeware and Browser Extension. Mod Settings optional (settings menu; without it: defaults).
ArchiveXL not needed (the site art archive only adds its own files). Compiles clean; untested in game (see below).

**Incompatible:** Missing Persons - Fixer's Hidden Gems (Nexus 5058) and Pacifica Typhoon - Dogtown's Hidden Gems
(Nexus 12245): same gems, they would pin and pay them twice. Remove them. Progress from them carries over (below).

## How it plays
- **Website** (any computer, `NETdir://nc.whispers`), two tabs. TODAY: N whispers per game day (default 3), posted on
  the first visit that day, each behind its own tip (rolled once, 50-300 eddies, saved); "Ask around" = one extra
  whisper, 100 doubling per ask, at most 5 asks, back to 100 24 game hours after the first ask. STILL OUT THERE: every open whisper,
  newest first, 4 per page. Cases closed + next payout.
- **Map pins**: the game's own shard icon (part `shard` of `mappin_icons.inkatlas`), set by wrapping the world map /
  minimap / in-world quest pin controllers' `UpdateIcon` for pins whose caption starts with `WhispersNC|`.
- **The street**: entering a district or stepping out of a vehicle rolls once (20 %) for the nearest hidden gem within
  250 m that is not on the map yet; at most one roll per 12 game hours.
- **Closing a case**: shard gems close when the shard lands in your journal (any time, revealed or not); Dogtown mail
  gems when you open that mail on a computer; Dogtown weapon gems when the game marks the iconic weapon as owned; spot
  gems (no shard) when V is at the revealed spot and picks something up, enters a district or steps out of a vehicle.
- **Rewards**: every 10 closed cases crafting components + 150 eddies; all of them 3000 + legendary components +
  Comrade's Hammer. Cases closed before the mod was installed count, but don't pay.
- **Gates**: city gems once "The Rescue" is done (`q001_mission0_complete`, set by the game in `q001_mission0.questphase`; Watson ones first, the rest after The Heist); Dogtown gems with Phantom
  Liberty installed (`IsEP1()`), the setting on, after `q301_done`. Per-gem prerequisites and "gone for good" rules use
  vanilla quest facts (all checked against the game's quest files).

- **Intro text**: at character level 5 (checked on the district / vehicle-exit triggers, after "The Rescue") the
  script-only contact WHISPERS texts V once about the site; the contact shows for 3 game days, read or not, then is
  gone. Skipped when the site was already opened (`wnc_day > 0`). Same no-journal technique as Boosting's BOOSTER.

## Layout
- `main/` - the mod, laid out like the game folder; `build.py` zips it into ONE download (no optional files)
  - `r6/scripts/WhispersNC/WNC_Core.reds` - state (facts `wnc_*`), reveal, completion events, pins + map tooltip,
    rewards, triggers (district / unmount / item added / computer mail wraps), soft JJ Bank / JJ Rewards hooks behind
    `ModuleExists` (the author's own mods; compiled out without them)
  - `WNC_Site.reds` - the website (Browser Extension)
  - `WNC_Phone.reds` - the one-shot WHISPERS phone contact (hash 0x574E4300, contact list + message panel wraps)
  - `WNC_ModSettings.reds` - Mod Settings menu (`WNCModSettings`) + `WNCCfg` (read at use, clamped)
  - `WNC_Data.reds` - GENERATED by `gen_data.py`
  - `archive/pc/mod/WhispersNC.archive` - site art (`whispers\gui\whispers.xbm` + `.inkatlas`, parts banner/tile/title)
- `data/gems.json` - the 240 gems (position, kind, journal title / fact, prerequisites, location text); made once by
  `tools/import_gems.py` (see its header: positions + shard ids are game facts, every title checked against the game
  journal, location text generated by us). `data/rumours.txt` - our rumour lines. `data/RUMOURS_review.txt` - all
  lines as shown, generated.
- `assets/source/logo.png` - the key art; `art.py` -> `assets/whispers_atlas.png`; `release/cover.png` = Nexus cover.

## Build
- `python gen_data.py` after editing `data/`.
- `python build.py [--deploy]` = regenerate + `dist/WhispersOfNightCity_v<version>.zip` (+ copy into the game).
- `python compat_test.py` = clean-install compile (vanilla + Codeware + Browser Extension (+ Mod Settings) + this mod).
- Art: `python archive.py` after changing `assets/source/logo.png`. Steps: `art.py` builds a 1024 x 1024 atlas png
  (banner 1024 x 576, tile 256 x 256, title 760 x 436); WolvenKit CLI imports it (`import -k`) onto a copy of the
  game's own UI texture `icons_atlas.xbm` (TEXG_Generic_UI, no mips, DXT), the game's `icons_atlas.inkatlas` becomes
  the structure with our three parts, `pack`, then segment / CRC / Oodle checks. Needs WolvenKit CLI + the game.

## State (facts)
`wnc_r<id>` reveal order, `wnc_d<id>` 1 closed / 3 gone, `wnc_rseq`, `wnc_day`, `wnc_post1..5` / `wnc_lk1..5` / `wnc_pp1..5` (today's
whispers, locked, tip price), `wnc_askn` / `wnc_askt` (ask-around count / window start), `wnc_ptime` (last street roll), `wnc_tier`, `wnc_all`, `wnc_init`, `wnc_intro` (0 not sent / 1 on the phone / 2 done) / `wnc_introt` (sent, seconds) / `wnc_intror` (read). First load: Missing Persons
`dark_hg_g<id>` / Pacifica Typhoon `dark_pt_g<id>` progress is read once (same gem order).

## Canary
`[WhispersNC] attach: ...` line in the CET / redscript log on every load.

## Not verified offline
- In game: nothing yet (WHISPERS text, site, pins, tooltip, reveal rolls, every completion path, the art).
- Gem positions are the community's coordinates (shard titles + facts are checked against game data, positions are
  not). Gem 71's shard is treated as removed by CDPR in 2.0 (never offered).
- Spot gems close only on an event at the spot (V picks something up there, district / vehicle exit); just walking
  past without one doesn't close them. Deliberate: no polling.
